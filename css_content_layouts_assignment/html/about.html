What is Easelful?

Easelful is a social media site made specially for creatives and artists across all levels of expertise. Easelful provides the ability to create highly customizable profiles where users can share their work in a place that feels true to them. Creatives can turn their profile into an interactive portfolio, journal, diary, book, or anything else! With Easeful, customizing your profile and sharing posts becomes a form of art.

Motivation

As someone who dabbles in various creative hobbies (journaling, painting, sketching, etc.), I have often wanted to share my work with others. However, I often felt that most social media sites did not grant me the level of creative freedom that I wanted, whether that was for my profile or for the posts I shared. My main motivation was to create a social site where my profile could really showcase who I am and, as a result, create a more genuine connection with others.

Mission

My mission is for Easelful to fill these gaps. Easelful aims to create a space for people to present their work to others in a way that feels true to them, and in doing so, allow people to connect more genuinely, find inspiration, and even learn from each other. Additionally, we hope that Easelful can provide a way for people to organize their creative work and look back on their creative journeys. We hope Easelful can serve as your art diary, journal, book, album, gallery, easel, or anything else you need it to be on your creative journey!
